# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

saves.fm is built by and for people who love retro games. Everyone who takes part, whether developer, designer, tester, translator or player, deserves to be treated with respect.

We are committed to making participation a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source, RetroAchievements, or both.
- Assume good intent.
- Give and receive feedback graciously, and keep it about the work.
- Celebrate every kind of player: softcore or hardcore, completionist or casual. Nobody's way of playing is the wrong one.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Gatekeeping or mocking how someone plays.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to the maintainer, [@fr0ggy-p3ngu1n](https://github.com/fr0ggy-p3ngu1n). All reports are handled confidentially, and you'll get a response within 72 hours.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately. Violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other place where community members represent the project.
